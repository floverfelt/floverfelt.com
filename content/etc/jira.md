[JIRA](https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira) is a piece of software that seems to slowly and inexporably rot people's brains.

It is the ultimate [bike shed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Law_of_triviality). It's _just_ complicated (and important!) enough that everybody has some opinion about how it should be used/managed/run/setup/color-scheme/UI/whatever, but at the end of the day it's a glorified to-do list, so it isn't *that* important and it doesn't actually create anything.

I think there's an element of the [manager and the maker](https://www.paulgraham.com/makersschedule.html) at play here. At a certain point, manager's lose the ability to actually *do* the things they're asking their employees to do. Sometimes, they never had that ability in the first place. And that's fine! But it does lead to higher-ups mistaking the tool-being-used-to-track-the-work with the work itself.

I'm sorry, but adding a new component label will _not_ help me get this [Jenkins](https://www.jenkins.io/) build done any quicker.
